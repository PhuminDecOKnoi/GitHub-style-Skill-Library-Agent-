# STOU 41402 — Thai and Foreign Legal Systems

## 1. Purpose

Use this skill for STOU 41402 study, comparative legal-system analysis, legal-history explanation, Thai Bar foundation alignment, legal mind mapping, and professional 16:9 legal mockup PNG work.

## 2. Scope

Covered topics include:

1. Thai legal-system development and legal reception.
2. Civil law, common law, religious law, mixed systems, and comparative frameworks.
3. Sources of law, codification, precedent, custom, and legal institutions.
4. Thai justice-system development and legal reform.
5. Comparative method and legal-system classification.
6. Study foundations useful for public law, private law, procedure, and Thai Bar orientation.

## 3. Source priority

Use STOU 41402 materials first; verify current legal-system or institutional claims when time-sensitive; use official Thai sources, foreign official sources, and reliable academic commentary for comparative points.

## 4. Core workflow

```text
Identify legal system or comparison target
→ classify system type
→ identify sources of law and institutions
→ compare method, authority, and legal reasoning
→ connect to Thai legal structure
→ identify study/exam relevance
→ conclude
→ add Thai Bar Alignment only when relevant
```

## 5. Thai Bar Alignment Protocol

This course is usually a foundation course, not a direct Thai Bar subject. Use Thai Bar alignment only as background for legal method.

```text
STOU 41402 topic
→ Thai Bar foundation mapping
→ legal method / source hierarchy
→ civil law / common law comparison
→ relevance to statutory interpretation and case-law use
→ exam-writing framework
→ checklist / next action
```

Core Thai Bar foundation value:

- statutory interpretation;
- civil-law codified reasoning;
- role of Supreme Court decisions;
- legal hierarchy;
- comparative caution when using foreign concepts.
