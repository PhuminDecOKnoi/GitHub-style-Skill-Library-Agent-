# STOU 41214 — Civil Law: Persons, Juristic Acts, and Obligations

## 1. Purpose

Use this skill for STOU 41214 study, Thai civil-law analysis, exam review, Thai Bar / เนติฯ alignment, legal mind mapping, and professional 16:9 legal mockup PNG work. The skill converts each unit into a source-grounded civil-law structure and, when relevant, a Thai Bar readiness block.

## 2. Scope

Covered topics include:

1. Persons, capacity, domicile, juristic persons, and status issues.
2. Juristic acts and declaration of intention.
3. Void and voidable acts.
4. Mistake, fraud, duress, sham intention, and concealed juristic acts.
5. Obligations and sources of obligations.
6. Performance, breach, default, damages, and extinction of obligations.
7. Contract formation and effects where relevant to juristic acts and obligations.

## 3. Source priority

Use this order:

1. User instruction and uploaded STOU unit material.
2. STOU 41214 course/unit materials.
3. Current Civil and Commercial Code text in force on the material date.
4. Supreme Court judgments or verified case-law anchors.
5. Thai Bar official materials when the task concerns เนติฯ.
6. Reliable academic commentary for explanation only.

Never invent provisions, case numbers, limitation periods, legal effects, or quotations.

## 4. Core workflow

```text
Identify issue
→ classify: person / juristic act / obligation / contract
→ identify governing provisions
→ break rule into elements and conditions
→ apply facts or unit content
→ test void/voidable effect, exception, limitation, and remedy
→ conclude
→ add Thai Bar Alignment only when useful
```
