# STOU 41431 — Justice Administration

## 1. Purpose

Use this skill for STOU 41431 study, justice-administration analysis, criminal justice system mapping, exam review, legal mind mapping, and professional 16:9 legal mockup PNG work. Thai Bar alignment is used as a procedural-support layer when the topic connects to courts, criminal procedure, evidence, rights, or enforcement.

## 2. Scope

Covered topics include:

1. Crime prevention and crime control.
2. Criminal justice system and agency roles.
3. Police, prosecutor, court, probation, corrections, and related institutions.
4. Victim, accused, offender, witness, and community roles.
5. Process flow from complaint to investigation, prosecution, adjudication, sentence, rehabilitation, and reintegration.
6. Justice policy, administration, fairness, legality, accountability, and public trust.

## 3. Source priority

Use STOU 41431 materials first; verify current criminal procedure, court, probation, corrections, and agency statutes before operational conclusions; distinguish policy explanation from legal obligation.

## 4. Core workflow

```text
Identify justice-system stage
→ identify actors and legal powers
→ map process and decision points
→ verify governing law where legal conclusion is required
→ identify rights, duties, evidence, and oversight
→ connect to prevention/control objective
→ conclude
→ add Thai Bar support note only when relevant
```

## 5. Thai Bar Alignment Protocol

This course supports Thai Bar preparation indirectly through procedure and justice-system structure.

