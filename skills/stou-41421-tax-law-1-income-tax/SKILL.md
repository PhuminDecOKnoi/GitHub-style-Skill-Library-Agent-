# STOU 41421 — Tax Law 1: Income Tax

## 1. Purpose

Use this skill for STOU 41421 study, Thai income-tax law analysis, exam review, Thai Bar / เนติฯ alignment, legal mind mapping, and professional 16:9 legal mockup PNG work.

## 2. Scope

Covered topics include:

1. General principles and structure of taxation.
2. Taxpayer, tax base, taxable income, deductions, allowances, rates, and computation where within the unit.
3. Personal income tax and corporate income tax foundations.
4. Withholding tax, assessment, filing, payment, surcharge, penalties, and appeal/remedy routes where relevant.
5. Interpretation of tax statutes and administrative practice.

## 3. Source priority

Use STOU 41421 materials first; verify current Revenue Code text, Royal Decrees, Ministerial Regulations, Revenue Department guidance, and court decisions before operative conclusions. Tax amounts, rates, thresholds, and deadlines are current-law sensitive.

## 4. Core workflow

```text
Identify tax issue and tax type
→ identify taxpayer and tax period
→ verify current Revenue Code and subordinate rules
→ identify income, exemption, deduction, rate, assessment, and remedy
→ apply facts or unit content
→ calculate only when data is sufficient
→ conclude
→ add Thai Bar Alignment only when relevant
```

## 5. Thai Bar Alignment Protocol

```text
STOU 41421 unit/topic
→ Thai Bar subject: ภาคหนึ่ง กฎหมายภาษีอากร
→ legal structure
→ current statutes
→ exam issues
→ verified case/decision anchor where available
→ exam-writing framework
→ checklist / next action
```

Core Thai Bar issues:

- tax subject and taxpayer;
- taxable income and exemption;
- assessment and filing duties;
- penalties, surcharge, appeal, and remedy;
- interpretation of tax statute and burden concerns.
