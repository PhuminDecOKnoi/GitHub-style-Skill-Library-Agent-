# STOU 41455 — Intellectual Property Law

## 1. Purpose

Use this skill for STOU 41455 study, Thai intellectual-property analysis, exam review, Thai Bar / เนติฯ alignment, legal mind mapping, and professional 16:9 legal mockup PNG work.

## 2. Scope

Covered topics include:

1. Copyright and related rights.
2. Patent, petty patent, and design patent issues.
3. Trademark, service mark, certification mark, and collective mark.
4. Trade secrets, geographical indications, plant varieties, layout-designs, and other IP where within the unit.
5. Ownership, transfer, licensing, infringement, exceptions, civil liability, criminal liability, administrative measures, border measures, evidence, and remedies.
6. International conventions and treaty context where included in the unit.

## 3. Source priority

Use STOU 41455 materials first; verify current statutes, amendments, subordinate legislation, Department of Intellectual Property sources, treaties, and verified judgments before legal conclusions.

## 4. Core workflow

```text
Classify IP subject matter
→ identify right creation or registration requirement
→ identify owner/right holder
→ define scope of exclusive right
→ test alleged act against infringement elements
→ test exception / limitation / exhaustion / licence
→ identify civil, criminal, administrative, or border consequence
→ conclude
→ add Thai Bar Alignment only when relevant
```

## 5. Thai Bar Alignment Protocol

```text
STOU 41455 unit/topic
→ Thai Bar subject: ภาคหนึ่ง กฎหมายทรัพย์สินทางปัญญา
→ legal structure
→ current statutes
→ exam issues
→ verified case/deka anchor where available
→ exam-writing framework
→ checklist / next action
```

Core Thai Bar issues:

- classification of IP right;
- creation/registration and ownership;
- scope of exclusive right;
- infringement elements;
- statutory exceptions;
- civil/criminal liability route;
- remedy and evidence;
- treaty or international-law context where relevant.

## 10. Guardrails

- Do not infer criminal liability from civil infringement without identifying the specific offence and penalty provision.
- Verify current amendments before using penalty figures or statutory exceptions.
- Do not create a standalone `/SKILL neti/bar *` workflow.
