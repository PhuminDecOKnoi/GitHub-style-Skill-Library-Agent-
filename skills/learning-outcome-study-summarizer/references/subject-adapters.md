# Subject adapters

Select only the adapter that matches the source. Combine adapters when a course is genuinely interdisciplinary.

## Law

Organize by legal issue, governing law, section or clause, elements, conditions, exceptions, legal effects, application, and disputed distinctions. Preserve the official title and provision number exactly as shown. Separate the source's legal rule from commentary, cases, and inferred application. Do not claim current validity unless the source or verified current research supports it.

## Business, management, HR, and audit

Organize by concept, objective, inputs, process, roles, tools, controls, outputs, measures, risks, and application. Distinguish descriptive models from mandatory requirements. Preserve standards, clauses, and KPI definitions.

## Social science and humanities

Organize by concept, theory, thinker or school when relevant, assumptions, causes, relationships, context, effects, comparison, critique, and application. Distinguish the author's position from competing explanations.

## Science and health science

Organize by definition, system, mechanism, causal chain, variables, evidence, result, limitation, and safe application. Preserve units and uncertainty. Do not turn educational material into personalized medical advice.

## Mathematics, statistics, accounting, and finance

Organize by definition, notation, assumptions, formula, variable meaning, method, necessary worked example, interpretation, and common error. Verify units, signs, rounding, and whether the method's conditions hold.

## Language and communication

Organize by rule or communicative goal, structure, vocabulary, usage, contrast, necessary example, and common error. Preserve source-language forms and add a concise explanation in the user's language when useful.

## Computing and technology

Organize by concept, architecture or components, data or control flow, procedure, interface, dependency, security or failure condition, and practical application. Retain only code needed to demonstrate the learning outcome and explain its function briefly.

## Practical, vocational, and procedural subjects

Organize by objective, prerequisites, materials or inputs, ordered steps, control points, safety constraints, acceptance criteria, evidence of completion, and common failure. Never omit a safety requirement to make a summary shorter.

## Interdisciplinary sources

Choose a primary adapter from the unit's learning outcomes. Apply a second adapter only to the relevant subsection. Keep mandatory rules, explanatory theory, calculations, and procedural steps visibly distinct.
