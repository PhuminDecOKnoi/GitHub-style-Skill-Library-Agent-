# STOU 41405 — Environmental Law

## 1. Purpose

Use this skill for STOU 41405 study, environmental-law analysis, current-law review, exam review, legal mind mapping, and professional 16:9 legal mockup PNG work. Thai Bar alignment is used only as an indirect support layer when environmental issues connect to public law, administrative law, tort, criminal law, or remedies.

## 2. Scope

Covered topics include:

1. Foundations and principles of environmental law.
2. Sustainable development, precautionary principle, polluter pays principle, public participation, and decentralization.
3. Environmental rights, community rights, and access to remedies.
4. Natural resources, biodiversity, pollution prevention, pollution control, and environmental impact processes.
5. Administrative, civil, and criminal enforcement.
6. Strict civil liability, damages, injunctions, restoration, and public-interest litigation where relevant.
7. International environmental principles and domestic implementation where included in the unit.

## 3. Source priority

Use STOU 41405 materials first; verify current environmental statutes, subordinate legislation, notifications, administrative court decisions, Supreme Court decisions, and competent agency sources before legal conclusions.

## 4. Core workflow

```text
Identify environmental issue
→ classify administrative / civil / criminal / constitutional / international layer
→ identify protected resource, pollutant, actor, authority, and affected right
→ verify governing law and current version
→ break rule into elements and procedure
→ apply facts or unit content
→ identify liability, remedy, and evidence
→ conclude
→ add Thai Bar foundation note only when relevant
```

## 5. Thai Bar Alignment Protocol

Environmental law is not a primary Thai Bar subject in the usual core mapping. Use alignment only when environmental law connects to:

- public law / administrative legality;
- tort and strict liability;
- criminal sanctions;
- constitutional rights and community rights;
- procedure and evidence.

```text
STOU 41405 unit/topic
→ supporting Thai Bar subject connection
→ legal structure
→ current statutes
→ exam/legal issues
→ verified case anchor where available
→ exam-writing or memo framework
→ checklist / next action
```

## 10. Guardrails

- Do not invent agency powers, EIA/EHIA requirements, standards, or penalty figures.
- Distinguish policy, principle, right, liability, and remedy.
- Do not create a standalone `/SKILL neti/bar *` workflow.
