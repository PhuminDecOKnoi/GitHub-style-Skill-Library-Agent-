# STOU 41404 — Labour Law

## 1. Purpose

Use this skill for STOU 41404 study, Thai labour-law analysis, HR/legal compliance understanding, exam review, Thai Bar / เนติฯ alignment, legal mind mapping, and professional 16:9 legal mockup PNG work.

## 2. Scope

Covered topics include:

1. Labour protection law.
2. Wages, overtime, holiday pay, rest, leave, welfare, and deductions.
3. Employment status, employer/employee relationship, and special worker groups.
4. Termination, severance, no-severance grounds, and unfair dismissal concepts where relevant.
5. Labour relations, collective bargaining, labour disputes, lockout, and strike.
6. Labour court and labour procedure basics.
7. Social security, workmen's compensation, OSH, and related special laws where within the unit scope.

## 3. Source priority

Use STOU 41404 materials and user-provided facts first; verify current statutes, regulations, ministerial rules, and official sources before legal conclusions; use verified judgments or official interpretations only when checked.

## 4. Core workflow

```text
Identify labour issue category
→ identify parties and employment status
→ verify controlling law and material date
→ break rule into statutory elements
→ apply facts or unit content
→ test exceptions, procedure, limitation, and remedy
→ state practical/exam conclusion
→ add Thai Bar Alignment only when relevant
```


Core Thai Bar issues:

- employer/employee status;

- statutory exceptions and remedies.
