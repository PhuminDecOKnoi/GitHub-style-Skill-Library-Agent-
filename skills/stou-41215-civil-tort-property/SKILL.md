# STOU 41215 — Civil Law: Tort and Property

## 1. Purpose

Use this skill for STOU 41215 study, tort/property analysis, exam review, Thai Bar / เนติฯ alignment, legal mind mapping, and professional 16:9 legal mockup PNG work.

## 2. Scope

Covered topics include:

1. Tort / unlawful act elements.
2. Intention, negligence, causation, damage, and compensation.
3. Liability for acts of others where relevant.
4. Property concepts, ownership, possession, and real rights.
5. Co-ownership, accession, acquisition and protection of ownership.
6. Remedies, limitation/prescription, and enforcement issues.

## 3. Source priority

Use user-provided sources and STOU 41215 materials first; then verify current Civil and Commercial Code provisions, verified judgments, Thai Bar official materials when relevant, and academic commentary for explanation only.

## 4. Core workflow

```text
Identify whether the issue is tort, property, or mixed
→ identify rights, acts, damage, property, and parties
→ verify governing provisions
→ break legal rule into elements
→ apply facts / unit content
→ test defences, limitation, and remedy
→ conclude
→ add Thai Bar Alignment only when relevant
```

```text
STOU 41215 unit/topic

→ legal structure

```

Core Thai Bar issues:

- tort elements and compensation;
- causation and damage;
- vicarious or special liability where applicable;
- ownership and possession;
- real rights and protection of property rights;
- acquisition of ownership;
- limitation and remedy.
