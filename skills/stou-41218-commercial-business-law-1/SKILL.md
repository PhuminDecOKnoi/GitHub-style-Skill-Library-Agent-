# STOU 41218 — Commercial and Business Law 1

## 1. Purpose

Use this skill for STOU 41218 study, Thai commercial-law analysis, exam review, Thai Bar / เนติฯ alignment, legal mind mapping, and professional 16:9 legal mockup PNG work.

## 2. Scope

Covered topics include commercial contracts and business relationships normally within Commercial and Business Law 1, including:

1. Sale and exchange where relevant.
2. Lease of property and lease effects.
3. Hire-purchase.
4. Agency and representation where included.
5. Deposit, carriage, insurance, or commercial obligations where included in the requested unit.
6. Rights, duties, liability, termination, remedies, and limitation.

## 3. Source priority

Use user-provided STOU 41218 material first; verify current Civil and Commercial Code provisions and special statutes before giving operative legal conclusions; use Thai Bar official materials only when the task is exam alignment.

## 4. Core workflow

```text
Identify contract type
→ identify parties, object, consideration, term, and breach
→ verify governing provisions
→ break rights/duties into elements
→ identify legal effect and remedy
→ check termination, limitation, and special rule
→ conclude
→ add Thai Bar Alignment only when relevant
```

```text
STOU 41218 unit/topic

```
