# STOU 41217 — Public Law and Constitutional Law

## 1. Purpose

Use this skill for STOU 41217 study, public-law and constitutional-law analysis, exam review, Thai Bar / เนติฯ alignment, legal mind mapping, and professional 16:9 legal mockup PNG work.

## 2. Scope

Covered topics include:

1. Nature and sources of public law.
2. State, sovereignty, organs of state, and exercise of state power.
3. Constitutional structure, legislature, executive, judiciary, and checks and balances.
4. Rights, liberties, duties, equality, and human dignity.
5. Rule of law, legality, public interest, and proportionality.
6. Constitutional review, administrative legality, and remedies where relevant.
7. Election, democracy, and public-law institutions when within the unit scope.

## 3. Source priority

Use user-provided STOU material first; verify the current Constitution, organic laws, administrative statutes, and official court decisions when giving legal conclusions; distinguish study doctrine from current law and political commentary.

## 4. Core workflow

```text
Identify state power / right / institution / procedure
→ classify constitutional or public-law issue
→ verify governing constitutional or statutory provision
→ identify public purpose and rights affected
→ apply legality, equality, and proportionality
→ identify remedy or review path
→ conclude
→ add Thai Bar Alignment only when relevant
```
