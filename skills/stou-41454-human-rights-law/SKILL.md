# STOU 41454 — Human Rights Law

## 1. Purpose

Use this skill for STOU 41454 study, human-rights law analysis, exam review, Thai Bar / เนติฯ support alignment, legal mind mapping, and professional 16:9 legal mockup PNG work.

## 2. Scope

Covered topics include:

1. Concept and development of human rights.
2. International human-rights law and UN/regional mechanisms.
3. Civil, political, economic, social, and cultural rights.
4. Rights in the criminal justice process.
5. Equality, non-discrimination, human dignity, limitation of rights, and proportionality.
6. Domestic protection, institutions, remedies, and implementation.
7. Rights of women, children, persons with disabilities, communities, and vulnerable groups where within the unit.

## 3. Source priority

Use STOU 41454 materials first; verify treaties, constitutional text, statutes, court decisions, and official human-rights sources before giving current-law conclusions. Distinguish international principles from directly enforceable domestic law.

## 4. Core workflow

```text
Identify right and affected person/group
→ identify state/private actor and legal duty
→ identify source of right: Constitution / statute / treaty / principle
→ test limitation, legality, legitimate aim, necessity, proportionality, non-discrimination
→ identify remedy or protection mechanism
→ conclude
→ add Thai Bar support alignment when relevant
```

## 5. Thai Bar Alignment Protocol

Human rights supports Thai Bar preparation especially for ภาคสอง and criminal justice process.

```text
STOU 41454 unit/topic
→ supporting Thai Bar subject: สิทธิมนุษยชนในกระบวนการยุติธรรม / วิ.อาญา / พยาน / รัฐธรรมนูญ
→ legal structure
→ current statutes or treaty source
→ exam issues
→ verified case/decision anchor where available
→ exam-writing framework
→ checklist / next action
```

Core support issues:

- rights of accused, victim, witness, and detainee;
- legality and due process;
- equality and non-discrimination;
- proportionality;
- effective remedy;
- status of treaties and domestic implementation.
