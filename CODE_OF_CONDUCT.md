# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a professional, respectful, inclusive, and evidence-based collaboration environment.

## Expected behaviour

- Communicate respectfully and constructively.
- Critique ideas, sources, methods, and code rather than individuals.
- Acknowledge uncertainty and correct errors transparently.
- Protect privacy, confidentiality, and legal privilege.
- Cite sources and distinguish evidence from opinion.
- Respect copyright, licensing, and attribution requirements.
- Disclose conflicts of interest that may affect legal or compliance analysis.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Deliberate misinformation or fabricated citations.
- Publishing personal, confidential, privileged, or sensitive data.
- Public disclosure of security vulnerabilities or credentials.
- Plagiarism or unauthorized reproduction of copyrighted material.
- Using the repository to target, intimidate, or unfairly profile workers or other individuals.

## High-risk domain conduct

Legal and HR discussions can affect employment, reputation, livelihood, and legal rights. Contributors must avoid presenting unreviewed AI output as final legal advice and must not use repository content to justify discriminatory, retaliatory, or unlawful action.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, reject, lock, or restrict content and participation when conduct conflicts with this policy. Responses should be proportionate to severity, repetition, and risk.

Report serious conduct or privacy concerns privately to the repository maintainer rather than through a public issue.
