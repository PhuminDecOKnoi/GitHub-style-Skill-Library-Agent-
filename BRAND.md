## Responsible reuse

Responsible reuse should:

- preserve the MIT copyright and permission notice;
- identify material modifications;
- avoid false claims of authorship or endorsement;
- preserve source traceability for legal and compliance content;
- respect third-party copyright, database, privacy, confidentiality, and attribution requirements;
- avoid presenting AI-generated legal outputs as verified professional conclusions.
