# Support Policy

## Usage questions

Use GitHub Discussions when enabled, or open a clearly scoped issue describing the file, workflow, expected outcome, and what has already been tried.

## Documentation and content errors

Use the content-error issue template. Include the affected path and section, current wording, proposed correction, source, date, and potential impact.

## Bugs and feature requests

Use the appropriate issue template and provide enough information for reproduction or evaluation. Remove confidential information and redact logs.

## Security and privacy

Do not open a public issue for vulnerabilities, exposed credentials, confidential documents, or personal-data incidents. Follow `SECURITY.md`.

## Legal and compliance limitations

The repository provides research and knowledge-management support only. Maintainers do not provide a service-level agreement, legal representation, or a guarantee that an answer is complete, current, or applicable to a particular dispute.

High-risk employment, litigation, disciplinary, compensation, and regulatory decisions require qualified human legal review.

## Response model

Support is provided on a best-effort basis. Response time and acceptance of requests depend on scope, risk, evidence quality, and maintainer capacity.
